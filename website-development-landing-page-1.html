Questions before you start?

Here are the important points covered by the agreement.

How quickly will my website be delivered?

The estimated delivery time is 5–7 business days from receipt of payment and all required client materials, information, approvals and access.

How many revisions are included?

Two reasonable revision rounds are included within the agreed project scope. New functionality, additional pages, new design directions or material scope changes may be charged separately.

What happens after the first year?

Recurring domain, hosting, premium plugin, software, API or third-party subscription costs after the included period are the client's responsibility unless otherwise agreed.

Who owns the completed website?

Upon receipt of all amounts due, ownership of the completed custom website work created specifically for the client transfers to the client, subject to the agreement's exclusions for pre-existing and third-party materials.

Does the package guarantee sales or leads?

No. The agreement does not guarantee search rankings, advertising performance, sales, leads, traffic or business results unless separately agreed in writing.
